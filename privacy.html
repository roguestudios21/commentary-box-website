Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 21, 2026

Welcome to Commentary Box! Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our iOS and Apple Watch applications ("App").

1. Information We Collect

Commentary Box is designed to be privacy first. To provide a personalized and secure experience, sign in is required for all users. However, we collect minimal data:

  • Authentication Data: We use Apple Sign In (for iOS) and Google Sign In (for Android) to authenticate you. We receive only basic profile information such as your email address necessary for account creation. We do not collect any additional personal data.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, and unique device identifiers to deliver accurate push notifications and Live Activities.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics about how you interact with the app, such as tournaments viewed and features used, to help us improve the experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect solely to:

  • Deliver real time cricket scores, push notifications, and Live Activities seamlessly to your iOS device and Apple Watch.
  • Ensure app stability and fix technical issues.
  • Improve the simplistic, distraction free design that Commentary Box is known for.

3. Third Party Services & Advertisements

We use trusted third party services to support our app's functionality, keep it free to download, and manage premium features:

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect and use data about your interactions with ads to provide targeted advertising.
  • RevenueCat: We use RevenueCat to manage and track in-app purchases and premium subscriptions. RevenueCat processes transaction data securely.
  • Other Services: We may use cloud infrastructure or analytics providers. These providers process data on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

4. Data Security

We use industry standard encryption and security practices to protect any information transmitted between our app and our servers.

5. Your Rights

You can manage push notification preferences and Live Activity permissions directly from your iOS settings at any time. If you wish to request the deletion of any data tied to your device identifier, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
