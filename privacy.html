Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 27, 2026

Welcome to Commentary Box! Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our iOS, Android, and Apple Watch applications ("App").

1. Data We Collect

The table below summarises every category of data we collect, whether it is linked to your identity, and whether it is used for tracking purposes.

Category Data Type Collected Linked to You Used for Tracking Source
Contact Info Name Yes Yes No Supabase (full_name)
Email Address Yes Yes No Supabase Auth
Identifiers User ID Yes Yes No Supabase (UUID)
Device ID Yes Yes Yes AdMob (IDFVs)
Purchases Purchase History Yes Yes No RevenueCat
Usage Data Advertising Data Yes Yes Yes AdMob
Product Interaction Yes Yes No Scores, Preferences

2. How We Use Your Data

Contact Info (Name & Email)

  • App Functionality: Used for user authentication and personalising your in-app profile. We do not use your name or email for marketing or share them with advertisers.

Identifiers (User ID & Device ID)

  • App Functionality: Your User ID (a UUID assigned by Supabase) is used to sync your data — including scores, preferences, and premium status — across your devices.
  • Third-Party Advertising: Your Device ID (IDFV) is shared with Google AdMob solely to serve advertisements within the app.
  • Analytics: RevenueCat and AdMob may use identifiers to monitor app performance and subscription health.

Purchases (Purchase History)

  • App Functionality: Purchase history is managed by RevenueCat to allow you to restore and manage your premium subscription. We do not store raw payment details on our servers.

Usage Data (Advertising Data & Product Interaction)

  • Third-Party Advertising: AdMob collects data about your interactions with ads (e.g. impressions and clicks) to serve relevant advertisements.
  • App Functionality: Game scores and team preferences are used to power features such as the leaderboard and personalised match feeds.

3. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following trusted third-party services. Each operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and user profile storage.
  • Google AdMob — in-app advertising. AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data for targeted advertising. Google Privacy Policy ↗
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase management and premium subscription tracking. RevenueCat Privacy Policy ↗

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect information transmitted between the app and our servers. All data stored in Supabase is protected with row-level security policies.

5. Your Rights & Choices

  • You can manage push notification and Live Activity permissions in your device settings at any time.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data directly from within the app or by contacting us.
  • To opt out of personalised advertising, use the privacy settings provided by your operating system (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this page regularly.
